The math, in the open

8 meetings × 5 attendees × 15 min = 600 person-minutes per week

600 min × 4.33 weeks ÷ 60 = 43.3 hours per month spent on notes

43.3 h × 80% recovered × $48/h = $1,663 per month in reclaimed time

$1,663 + $100 replaced tool spend = $1,763 saved per month

$3,000 build ÷ $1,763/mo × 30.4 days = pays for itself in 52 days

Year one: $16,353 net after build price and 12 months of maintenance.

Why 80%, not 100%. People still skim their notes - that is the point of notes. We only claim the time MeetingOS genuinely hands back: writing, formatting, sending, and chasing. Quoting 100% would be a sales number. Quoting 80% is a number you can defend in front of the owner's bookkeeper.

The money is the small half. The hours come back as mornings that start with decisions already made instead of inboxes full of "remind me what we agreed." Price the money honestly; let the client feel the time and energy return in week one.
