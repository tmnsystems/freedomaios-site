This is a working session, not a sales call. You get on the phone with a senior FreedomAIOS strategist for 60 minutes. How the 60 Minutes Work

  1. Before the call. You answer a short questionnaire about your business. Your strategist reviews every answer before you ever get on the phone, so no minute of the call is spent on basics.
  2. The baseline. You establish where the business actually stands, in real numbers. Historical data does not flatter anyone.

  4. The pipeline map. You lay out how work and money move through the business. Most owners have never seen this in front of them.
  5. The roadmap. You agree on what is broken and the order to fix it. The written Freedom Roadmap follows the call.
