The Five Steps of the Audit

The audit follows a five-step process. The internal steps stay veiled. They are the product.

The shape of the process is this:

  1. Baseline. We establish where you actually stand, not where you think you stand. Historical data does not flatter anyone.
  2. Effective hourly rate. We calculate what your time is actually worth and where it is actually going. The gap between those two numbers is usually the first shock.
  3. Pipeline visibility. We map how work and money actually move through your business. Most owners have never seen this laid out in front of them.
  4. The two-column sort. Every activity in your business goes into one of two columns. The sort is brutal and it is clarifying.
  5. The Dashboard of Truth. The final deliverable. Your roadmap, on paper: what is broken, where the leaks are, what to fix first.

If you've got blanks in core fields, you've got unreliable data, and building a strategy on unreliable data is like building a house on a foundation you never checked. That's why we start with the baseline. Then we calculate your effective hourly rate: the hours you work each month, divided into the money you're making. That number tells you what your hour is actually worth, and for most owners it's the first real shock of the day.
